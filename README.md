# APEX-SUPERMARKET-ANALYSIS


## 📊 Project Overview

This dashboard presents an end-to-end sales analysis of **Apex Supermarket** across three branches — **Branch A (Yangon), Branch B (Mandalay), and Branch C (Naypyitaw)**. Built in Power BI, it transforms raw transactional data into an interactive tool for uncovering revenue trends, customer behaviour, and branch-level performance to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🔍 Key Findings

**💰 Revenue Performance**
Apex Supermarket generated a total of **$322,970** in revenue across **1,000 transactions**, giving an average transaction value of roughly $323 — a useful benchmark for evaluating basket size and upsell opportunities.

**🏆 Branch Performance**
**Branch C (Naypyitaw)** led the network, outperforming both Yangon and Mandalay on revenue *and* customer satisfaction. This dual strength suggests Branch C's success isn't just about foot traffic or pricing — service quality and customer experience are likely reinforcing each other there, making it a strong internal benchmark for the other two branches.

**🛍️ Product Line Insights**
**Electronic Accessories** was the top-selling category overall, pointing to strong demand for tech-adjacent, high-margin items — a signal worth exploring for targeted promotions or inventory prioritization.

**📅 Weekly Traffic Patterns**
**Saturday** recorded the highest sales volume across the week, consistent with typical weekend shopping behaviour. This kind of pattern is directly actionable — it can inform staffing schedules, restocking timing, and weekend-specific promotions.

**📉 Seasonal Trend**
**February recorded the lowest sales** of any period in the dataset, marking it as a seasonal low point. This is worth flagging for further investigation — whether it reflects a post-holiday spending dip, fewer shopping days, or a branch-specific slump — and it's a natural entry point for testing off-season promotions or loyalty incentives.

**💳 Payment Preferences**
**Ewallet** was the most popular payment method, closely followed by **Cash** and **Credit Card**. The near-even split across three payment types shows a diversified customer base and reinforces the importance of maintaining robust support for all three channels rather than over-investing in one.

**⭐ Customer Satisfaction**
The average customer rating across all branches was **6.97/10** — a solid but not exceptional score, suggesting room for service improvements, particularly at the lower-performing branches relative to Branch C.

## 🗂️ Dashboard Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Branch Comparison** | Revenue and rating breakdown across Branches A, B, and C |
| **Product Line Analysis** | Sales performance by product category |
| **Payment Insights** | Payment method preferences by city and branch |
| **Day-of-Week Trends** | Sales volume patterns across the week |
| **Seasonal Trends** | Monthly sales patterns, highlighting February as the low point |
| **Customer Ratings** | Average satisfaction scores per branch |

---

 ## general recommendation

Apex Supermarket should use Branch C (Naypyitaw) as its internal performance benchmark and replicate its practices network-wide, while pairing that with a seasonal promotion strategy to smooth out the February dip and consistent stocking/staffing aligned to weekly demand patterns (like the Saturday peak).
